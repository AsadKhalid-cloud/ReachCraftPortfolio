CASE STUDY #01 · WEB DEVELOPMENT & BRANDING

Transforming an Offline Business Into a Modern Digital Brand

The Challenge

The client relied only on word-of-mouth and had no website or professional social presence, losing online customers to competitors.

Our Solution

We designed a complete brand identity, built a fast mobile-first website with one-click WhatsApp inquiries, and optimized their Google presence.

The Impact

A 100% professional online storefront that loads in under 2 seconds and generates direct customer inquiries on WhatsApp every week.

CASE STUDY #02 · META ADS + AI VIDEO CREATIVES

Lowering Ad Costs With High-Converting Video Creatives

The Challenge

The business was boosting basic static image posts on Facebook with poor targeting, getting likes but zero actual buyers.

Our Solution

We produced engaging AI video ads & edited Reels with strong hooks, and launched structured WhatsApp lead campaigns via Meta Ads Manager.

The Impact

Stopped wasted ad spend and turned social media into a predictable lead-generation channel with high-intent WhatsApp chats.
